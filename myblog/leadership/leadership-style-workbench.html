        A read you cannot cite is a guess. Log what you actually observed and mark it confirmed or inferred, the same discipline you would apply to a finding. Confidence rises with confirmed observations and stalls on inference alone.

        The most durable artefact of managing upward is a short written agreement on decision rights. Draft it yourself and hand it over — most leaders will happily edit a straw man and never author one. Revisit it after the first thing goes wrong, because that is when you find out where the real line was.
