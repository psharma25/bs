AI Governance Is Not Data Governance

They overlap, and one depends on the other, but they answer different questions and fail in different ways. What follows: where the line actually sits, what a good AI policy does, the mistakes I see most often, the pre-work worth doing before you write a word, and a model policy you can adapt.

The views expressed here are my own. They do not represent the views of any employer, client, or other organisation with which I am or have been associated, and nothing here should be taken as legal advice.

The model policy is a starting point, not a compliant document. It has to be adapted to your sector, your jurisdictions and your contractual obligations before use, and reviewed by someone qualified to do so.
