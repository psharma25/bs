Security, AI, and the People Who Have to Live With Both

Control maps you can work from, a model policy you can adapt, and the part everyone skips — why people do what they do when the tooling is not watching. Everything here is actionable, nothing here is vendor marketing.

Where to start. If the question is “should we turn Copilot on”, or “how do we share data without it training somebody’s model”, start with AI security. If it is “what do we test” or “where do we really get hacked”, start with the controls library and manufacturing security. Pick the section that matches your next conversation.
