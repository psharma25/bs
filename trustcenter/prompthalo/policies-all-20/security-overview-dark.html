Security Overview

How PromptHalo protects customer information, and which documents are available on request

About this page. This is a summary, not a policy – where it summarises a control the underlying policy governs, and the documents listed below are either published in full on this Trust Center or released under NDA on request at security@prompthalo.ai.

In Brief

PromptHalo Technologies builds security testing tooling for AI and agentic systems. The Company is small and deliberately runs on a narrow, managed technology footprint rather than a broad one, which is the single largest factor in the shape of the controls described here.

  • The security program is aligned to the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria for Security. The Privacy category is out of scope for the current examination, a decision recorded in the risk register and revisited when a customer agreement requires it.

  • Customer data is never used to train or fine-tune models, whether the Company’s own or a provider’s.

  • The Company runs no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of any kind on its site or platform.

  • A published coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy with safe harbour applies to good-faith research, and reports receive a response from a person within two business days.

Governance

The Chief Executive Officer is the Accountable Party for the security program and owns every policy in the library. The Security Officer is accountable for day-to-day operation and for privacy alongside security. At the Company’s current size the Information Security Steering Committee comprises the CEO, and its decisions are satisfied by a dated written record rather than a formal meeting – this is stated openly in the policies rather than dressed up as a committee that does not exist.

Every policy carries an owner, a version, an effective date, an annual review date, a classification and a distribution level. Policies are reviewed annually or on material change, and each carries a document history.

Identity and Access

Google Workspace is the identity provider. Access is granted on least privilege, requires multi-factor authentication, is reviewed on the cadence set in the Access Control Policy, and is removed on separation. Personnel with access to personal data are screened, bound by confidentiality terms, and complete privacy and security awareness training.

Encryption and Data Handling

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or better, and at rest using AES-256. Information is classified under a four-tier scheme – Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted – with handling rules attached to each. Restricted information is not transmitted over unencrypted channels.

Production personal data is not used in development or test environments unless it has been de-identified. Where that is not practicable the use is approved by the Accountable Party and recorded with a review date.

Retention periods are set in the Data Retention and Disposal Policy. Where a period ends, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised; backups are overwritten on their own cycle and deleted records are not restored to active use.

Infrastructure and Change

The platform runs on AWS. The Company does not operate its own data centres. Changes follow the Change Management Policy, and software is built under the Secure Software Development Policy, which covers review, testing and release.

Monitoring and Response

Authentication, administrative actions and access to Company systems are logged and monitored, so that unauthorised access is capable of being detected rather than assumed not to have happened. Alerts route to a monitored security address and are triaged on a defined cadence.

Security events are handled under the Incident Response Policy, which sets roles, escalation and a notification matrix governing who is told and by when. Where the Company acts as a processor, the controller is notified without undue delay. Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to an individual’s rights, affected people are told without undue delay.

Vulnerability Management

Vulnerabilities are tracked and remediated under the Vulnerability and Patch Management Policy. Externally reported issues are handled under Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Policy, which is published in full, sets out safe harbour for good-faith research, and states remediation timelines by severity. Machine-readable contact details are published at https://prompthalo.ai/.well-known/security.txt.

Resilience

Backup, recovery objectives and restoration testing are set in the Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy. Recovery objectives are stated in that document, which is available under a non-disclosure agreement.

Vendors and Sub-processors

Vendors are assessed before engagement and reassessed under the Vendor Management Policy. Each has a named internal owner. Sub-processors handling customer personal data are recorded on a sub-processor list, and customers are notified before a new sub-processor begins processing their data, on the terms of the engagement agreement. The current list is available on request.

Privacy

The Company does not sell personal data and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as US state privacy law defines those terms. Personal data is disclosed only to service providers acting on documented instructions under written contract.

Where the Company processes customer data, the customer is the controller and the Company is the processor: it acts only on documented instructions and does not use the data for any purpose of its own. The published Privacy Notice and Cookie Policy set out the full position.

AI Handling

This is the question customers ask first, so it is answered plainly.

  • Customer data is not used to train or fine-tune any model, the Company’s own or a provider’s.

  • Where a commercial AI service is used in delivery, it is used under business or enterprise terms that exclude submissions from provider training.

  • No decision producing legal or similarly significant effects is made about an individual by automated means alone. A named person is accountable for any output acted upon.

  • AI use is governed by AI Governance Policy, which is published in full.

Document Availability

Documents are classified for sensitivity and separately assigned a distribution level. Anything marked Read online opens directly, with no request and no gate. Anything marked Under NDA is released in full on request – the link opens an email to the security desk, and documents are usually returned within two business days once a non-disclosure agreement is in place. You can also request them through the Document requests panel on the Trust Center, which keeps a local record of what you asked for.
