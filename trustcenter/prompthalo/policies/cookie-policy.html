01What this covers

This policy explains how PromptHalo Technologies uses cookies and similar technologies on prompthalo.com and on the signed-in PromptHalo platform.

It sits alongside our Privacy Notice, which explains everything else we do with personal information. Where this policy uses a term the Privacy Notice defines, it carries the same meaning.

02What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser and reads back on later requests. Related technologies — local storage, session storage, and pixels — work differently but serve similar purposes, and this policy covers those too.

Cookies are described as strictly necessary when a service cannot function without them: keeping you signed in, protecting a form submission, or sending your request to the server holding your session. Under the ePrivacy Directive and comparable U.S. rules, strictly necessary cookies do not require consent. Every other category does.

03Our approach

PromptHalo sets strictly necessary cookies only. We have made a deliberate decision not to run analytics, product telemetry cookies, marketing pixels, session replay, or any third-party tracker on our site or in our platform.

That decision has a practical consequence worth stating plainly: because we set nothing that requires consent, you will not see a cookie banner on prompthalo.com, and there is no preference centre to configure. The absence of a banner is not an oversight.

We measure service reliability from server-side logs described in our Privacy Notice, not from cookies placed in your browser.

05Third-party cookies

We embed no third-party trackers. One third party does appear in the sign-in flow.

Google Workspace is our identity provider. When you sign in, you are handed to Google to authenticate, and Google sets its own cookies on its own domains to manage that. Those cookies are governed by Google's privacy and cookie policies, not by this one, and we neither set nor read them. We receive only the identity assertion confirming you signed in successfully.

If we ever introduce a third-party cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will add consent controls before it is set, and update this policy first.

06What we do not use

  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics or any equivalent
  • Advertising, retargeting, or conversion pixels
  • Cross-site or cross-context behavioral tracking of any kind
  • Session replay or heatmap tools
  • Social media embeds that set cookies
  • Fingerprinting, or any technique used to identify your device without cookies

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as California law defines those terms. We have no cookie-based data to sell.

07Controlling cookies

Every major browser lets you view, block, and delete cookies through its settings, and offers a private browsing mode that discards them when the window closes.

Because we set only strictly necessary cookies, blocking them has a direct effect: you will not be able to sign in to the platform, and it will not work. The public pages will still be readable.

We honor Global Privacy Control and comparable browser-level opt-out signals. Since we run no tracking, these signals confirm what we already do rather than change our behaviour.

08Changes to this policy

If we add, remove, or change a cookie, we update this policy and the table in section 4 before the change reaches production. Adding anything beyond strictly necessary cookies would also mean introducing consent controls, and we would tell account administrators before doing so.
